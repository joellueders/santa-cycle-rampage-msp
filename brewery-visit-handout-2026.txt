SANTA CYCLE RAMPAGE
Would your brewery like to be a stop?

Saturday, December 5, 2026
Minneapolis

Santa Cycle Rampage is a free holiday bike ride through Minneapolis. Riders dress as Santa, decorate their bikes, and stop at local breweries along the way. We also collect new, unwrapped toys for kids in need, with gifts planned for The Salvation Army.

We’re visiting breweries as we plan this year’s route. We’d love to talk about including yours.

What a stop would involve

A group of festive cyclists arriving together, parking their bikes, and spending time at your taproom. Riders buy their own food and drinks. Not everyone drinks alcohol.

The route is still being planned. We would agree on an arrival time, how long we stay, and an expected group size with you before confirming your stop.

What we’d like to discuss

Can your taproom handle a large group on December 5?
What arrival time would work best?
Where could riders park their bikes without blocking entrances or sidewalks?
Are there other events or staffing needs we should plan around?
Are you already running a holiday toy drive?
Could you be our gift collection location and arrange delivery to The Salvation Army, or help coordinate the handoff?
Who should we contact to confirm the details?

We plan to share confirmed stops on our website and in our event posts. Our target is to settle the lineup by the end of October.
