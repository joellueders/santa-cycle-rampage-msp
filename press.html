A short description

Twin Cities Santa Cycle Rampage is a free annual holiday bicycle ride through Minneapolis. Started in 2013 with ten friends, the ride drew 178 participants in 2025. Riders dress like Santa, decorate their bikes, visit local breweries, and carry new, unwrapped toys to donate to the Salvation Army.

Three ways into the story

  • A little unexpected joyPeople stop their errands, roll down their windows, wave and smile as a sea of Santas rides past. The city gets to be part of the fun.
  • Yes, they bike to breweries in DecemberSanta suits, decorated bicycles and local taprooms make a Minnesota winter day worth getting outside for.
  • Their bikes are their sleighsRiders carry new, unwrapped toys through Minneapolis to donate to kids in need.
