For introductions and listings

Copy-paste description

Twin Cities Santa Cycle Rampage is an annual holiday bicycle ride through Minneapolis. Riders dress as Santa or other festive characters, decorate their bikes, visit local breweries, bring toys to donate and spread cheer across the city.

Ready for the headline

Four ways into the story

  1. Yes, they really bike in December

    Nearly 200 costumed riders take to Minneapolis streets in the middle of a Minnesota winter.

    How a small ride among friends grew into a Minneapolis holiday tradition. A tradition grows up with its riders. More than a decade later, longtime riders still come back, and some now bring their kids.

  2. Santas bring toys, too

    Riders carry toys on their bikes to donate to kids in need.

  3. Local craft beer, ridiculous Santas

    A brewery-to-brewery bike ride celebrating Minneapolis taprooms and local craft beer.

  4. From Santa mayhem to Minneapolis cheer.

    The Santa Rampage started with San Francisco pranksters, turned into SantaCon, and ended up on bicycles in Minneapolis.
