The whole idea

Ride a bike.
Drink a beer.
Spread some cheer.

Santa Cycle Rampage MSP is a free holiday bike ride through Minneapolis. We dress as Santas, elves, and reindeer. We decorate our bikes and bring toys for kids in need. Then we ride together from brewery to brewery and give the people we pass a little unexpected joy.

A little joy in the streets.
The ride gives back

Bringa gift.

Toys for kids in need.

Bring a toy for kids in need and carry it with you on your bike. We’ll collect donations at a brewery stop along the route.

Your bike is your sleigh.

How it works

01

Show up

Meet at The Loop at 10 AM for breakfast. When it fills up, there are other places nearby. Many riders skip breakfast and join at the first brewery, our second stop. For them, that is where the day begins.

02

Ride together

Take it slow. Stay with the group and look out for each other. Bring bells and make some noise. Bring front and rear bike lights. By the last two stops, the sun will be going down.

03

Drink a beer

We stop at breweries along the way. Have a local craft beer and enjoy the company. You do not have to drink.

04

Spread some cheer

Wave to the people on the sidewalk and watching from their cars. Ring your bell. Give them something to smile about. Keep riding.

FAQ

Do I need to register?

No. The ride is free and there is no registration. Show up and ride. You can RSVP on Facebook—it’s optional, but appreciated!

Do I have to dress like Santa?

Dress festive. Santa is traditional, but the photographic record contains elves, reindeer, gingerbread people, Christmas trees and stranger things.

Do I have to drink?

No. Brewery stops are social stops, not drinking requirements. Ride in whatever way makes the day work for you.

Can I come if I don’t ride?

Absolutely. Meet us at an announced brewery stop, or travel between stops by car. Some Santas do. Bring friends for lunch and surprise them when we arrive. Arrange a sober driver if you’re drinking.

Can I join partway through?

Yes. Meet the group at any brewery stop. Many riders join at the first brewery after breakfast at The Loop.

What if there's snow?

It's Minneapolis in December. Dress for the weather and make your own call about riding conditions and your bike setup.

Can I bring a toy to donate?

Yes. We collect toys for kids in need at a brewery along the route. Bring yours by bike, like Santa carrying presents in his sleigh. We’ll announce the collection stop closer to ride day.

Are kids allowed?

Yes. Plan for roughly 15 miles if you ride the full day, so kids need a parent or guardian. Some families ride with kids in Burley trailers; others join for one or two stops. Ride at your own risk and follow traffic laws.

Is this a race?

Absolutely not. It's a social group ride.
