The whole idea

What is this?

We ride bicycles from brewery to brewery across Minneapolis, dressed like Santas. But the destination is the people we meet along the way.

A little absurdity, a little beer, a gentle disregard for social norms, and a whole lot of bicycles, kindness, and holiday cheer.

JOIN THE RESISTANCE.

Work. Buy. Scroll. Repeat. Keep your head down. Keep to yourself.

Fudge that! There’s something about wearing a Santa suit that makes it easier to be kind to strangers and makes strangers more willing to accept that kindness.

The suit helps them lower their guard, while being part of a whole ridiculous group of Santas lets us lower ours and be openly joyful.

We bring toys for kids and ride from brewery to brewery through Minneapolis, but the people we meet are the destination.

That’s our particular brand of mayhem: in a weary world, kindness is its own kind of rebellion.

FAQQuestion everything.

Do I need to register?

No. The ride is free and there is no registration. Show up and ride. You can RSVP on Facebook, it’s optional, but appreciated!

No tickets. No registration. The ride is free. Show up and ride. An RSVP on Facebook is optional, but appreciated. That’s the entire entrance exam.

Can I come alone?

Yes. You don’t need to know anyone to join us. Come on your own or bring a friend. Put on your Santa suit, say hello, and ride with the group.

Yes. You don’t need a crew or an introduction. Put on your Santa suit, say hello, and ride with us. We’re all strangers until somebody waves first.

Do I have to dress like Santa?

A Santa suit is the idea, and most of us wear one. You’re still welcome if you turn up as something else. We’ve had Christmas trees, Krampus, and a whole assortment of characters from the old stop-motion Christmas specials.

A Santa suit is the idea, and most of us wear one. But a Christmas tree, Krampus, or a character from the old stop-motion Christmas specials can join the mayhem too. You’re welcome as something else. Give us something to grin at.

What if there's snow?

We’ve ridden through snowstorms, ice, freezing rain and, unfortunately, increasingly warm weather. Christmas is starting to feel like Christmas in July. Yikes.

It’s Minneapolis in December, so dress appropriately. We’re seeing more and more fat bikes on the ride these days. They’ve become pretty common. If there’s snow or ice, studded tires are recommended.

Ultimately, ride only in conditions you’re comfortable with, and make sure your bike is set up accordingly. Even in the coldest years, people often start shedding layers by midday.

You may see Santas ducking into brewery bathrooms to peel off sweaty sweatpants beneath their costumes.

Turns out Santa suits don’t breathe particularly well.

Are kids allowed?

Sorta. Kids need to be with a parent or guardian, but this isn’t an event made for kids. We pride ourselves on not being rowdy, but it’s still a winter brewery ride with adults drinking. The full ride is about 15 miles, conditions can be icy, and the last stops are after dark.

If you bring kids, use your judgment about the conditions and distance. Joining for one or two stops earlier in the day may be a better fit than the full ride. If you’re driving between stops, stay sober.

Ride at your own risk and follow traffic laws.

Can I come if I don't ride a bicycle?

Absolutely. Check the schedule and meet your friends at a brewery. You can join the roomful of Santas without riding between stops.

Or invite some unsuspecting friends out for lunch at one of our stops. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes before we do.

Don’t tell them about the Santas. Let us be the surprise.
