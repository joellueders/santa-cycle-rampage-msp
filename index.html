What is this?

Santa Cycle Rampage MSP is a free holiday bike ride through Minneapolis. Riders dress as Santas, elves, and reindeer, decorate their bikes, bring gifts to donate to children in need, and ride together from brewery to brewery. Along the way, they spread unexpected cheer to everyone they pass.

FAQ

Do I need to register?

No. The ride is free and there is no registration. Show up and ride. You can RSVP on Facebook—it’s optional, but appreciated!

Do I have to dress like Santa?

Dress festive. Santa is traditional, but the photographic record contains elves, reindeer, gingerbread people, Christmas trees and stranger things.

Do I have to drink?

No. Brewery stops are social stops, not drinking requirements. Ride in whatever way makes the day work for you.

Can I come if I don’t ride?

Absolutely. Meet us at an announced brewery stop, or travel between stops by car. Some Santas do. Bring friends for lunch and surprise them when we arrive. Check the posted schedule for approximate times, and arrange a sober driver if you’re drinking.

Can I join partway through?

Yes. Once the stop schedule is published, use the approximate times to meet the group at a stop. The exact riding route between stops is not published.

What if there's snow?

It's Minneapolis in December. Dress for the weather and make your own call about riding conditions and your bike setup.

Can I bring a toy to donate?

Yes. We collect toys for kids in need at a brewery along the route. Bring yours by bike, like Santa carrying presents in his sleigh. We’ll announce the collection stop closer to ride day.

Are kids allowed?

Yes. The full ride can be more than 10 miles, so kids need a parent or guardian. Some families ride with kids in Burley trailers; others join for one or two stops. Ride at your own risk and follow traffic laws.

Is this a race?

Absolutely not. It's a social group ride.
