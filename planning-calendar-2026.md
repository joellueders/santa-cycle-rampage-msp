## Repeatable weekly rhythm

- Monday: 20-minute organizer review—venue follow-ups, unresolved decisions, next deadlines.
- Tuesday: photo/video or story post—joy, history, costumes, recognizable Minneapolis scenes.
- Thursday: useful post—confirmed stops, toy drive, joining information, or preparation.
- Sunday: 30-minute batch preparation for the next two posts; record reach, shares, comments, event responses, website visits where available, and paid spend/results separately.
- From Nov 16: add a Saturday countdown or partner spotlight if there is a distinct message.
- Final week: three planned posts plus material updates; avoid duplicating every message across channels without a reason.

This is a manageable starting rhythm, not a claim about optimal Facebook posting times.
